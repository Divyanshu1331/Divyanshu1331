### 🔬 Current Research

**Independent research (solo, unsupervised):** I chose the problem, designed the empirical study, ran it, and wrote it up entirely on my own — no lab, no advisor, no co-authors.

- Conducted a controlled empirical study on Vision Transformers in small-data regimes
- Showed optimization strategy (warmup + cosine decay) matters more than architectural scaling for generalization
- Achieved ~71% accuracy on CIFAR-10 vs. ~45–55% baseline
- **Paper accepted at SciForDL (ICLR 2026 Workshop)** — [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BnKObGDEr6) · [Code](https://github.com/Divyanshu1331/optimization-not-architecture-vit)
- Wrote up the full study as a standalone blog post: [*Why ViT Struggles on Small Datasets: A 4-Stage Experiment on CIFAR-10*](https://divyanshu1331.medium.com/why-vit-struggles-on-small-datasets-a-4-stage-experiment-on-cifar-10-7634ab2ae94c)
